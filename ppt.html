Now: how do we charge so we never lose money on a tenant?

Price every package above its worst-case cost, and break even on platform at one tenant

Platform break-even

0.90 Starter tenants cover the pilot platform (Rp1.0 jt). 1.92 Growth tenants cover the growth platform (Rp4.5 jt). Team salaries are the real break-even, not modelled here.

Why not pay-as-you-go

Visible per-use cost makes customers ration, and partial use breaks balances. Fixed quota means use is free at the margin.

Two conditions

Meta must bill Avara for WhatsApp to be included (Q16). Prices are untested with buyers (Q22).

Sources: PRD v3.1 §16.1 to §16.8, §16.4 (break-even), §2.3 (adoption). Indicative prices excl. PPN; top-up Rp750 per credit; pilot at worst-case cost (Rp1.3 jt or Rp3.9 jt a month).

Future: what ships when, with the team we have?

Ship a pilot slice by 31 December, then harden through January

Pilot slice by 31 Dec

  • Audit log, no-delete rules, gapless numbering from day one
  • Orders on WhatsApp with a confirm button; delivery notes with "Sudah diterima"
  • Invoices with pay links; bank matching with balance checks
  • Digest reminders; push only; one branch, three roles

Why the audit trail comes first

Records created before the trail exists can never be made trustworthy later. Reports, close and auditor access can wait because they read data that is already sound.
