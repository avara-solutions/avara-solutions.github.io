What Avara does

An assistant with real write access, on a short leash.

Avara isn't a chatbot that answers questions and hands off. It performs the work in your records, and it does it the same way a careful staff member would: check first, act once, log everything.

Orders straight from the chat

Customers order in their own words or send a PO photo. Avara matches your catalog, prices it from their agreement, echoes it back with "Ya, benar" and "Ubah", and checks their credit limit before confirming.

Invoices and payment links

Invoices from what was actually delivered, with numbers that can't collide. The PDF arrives in the thread with a secure link where the customer sees the invoice and pays by transfer, or by VA, QRIS and e-wallet where DOKU is enabled.

Payments matched from your bank statement

Upload your internet-banking statement. Transfers are matched automatically when the reference or amount is clear; the rest are suggested to finance. Giro and cash collected by your salesmen or drivers are tracked until they clear the bank. A screenshot is never proof of payment.

Invoice reminders that stay polite

One reminder per customer listing everything due, sent inside WhatsApp's template rules. When a customer says "Jumat saya transfer", the promise is recorded and the next reminder waits for that date.

Credit status and approvals

Every customer gets a rule-based status (OK, Watch, Hold) with the reason shown. Orders over the limit or on Hold wait for an approver, and nothing moves until someone on your team decides.

It never loses its place

If a step fails halfway, Avara retries only that step, so there are no duplicate invoices. If the AI is unavailable, customers get a simple menu instead of silence, and your team is told. Unclear sends are reviewed, never blindly resent.

Trust & safety

It touches money and invoices. It's built like it.

An assistant with write access is only useful if it's predictable. These aren't features you configure. They're how Avara is put together.

Everyone sees only their part

Distributors can't see each other, and inside your company each person sees only their role, branch or assigned customers. It's enforced in the database row by row, with two-step login for owners, finance and approvers.

Signatures verified before anything happens

Every WhatsApp and payment notification is cryptographically verified before a single record is touched. Unsigned requests are rejected at the door.

PDFs are built in a sealed box

Invoice rendering runs isolated, with no internet access and no credentials, so a malicious document can't reach anything it shouldn't.

Paid means paid

An invoice is marked paid only from your bank statement or a verified payment notification, each counted once. A screenshot in the chat never marks anything paid, and links sent over WhatsApp carry no passwords.

Nothing happens off the record

Every tool execution is stored with its input, output and outcome. It is the same log your ops team reads when a customer disputes something.

A human is always one message away

When a request is unusual, ambiguous, or fails validation twice, Avara hands the conversation to your team and tells the customer someone will reply shortly. It doesn't guess.

Built for your auditor

Every number traces back to evidence.

When your auditor tests the sales cycle, they ask the same questions every year: did this sale really happen, is anything missing, was it in the right period, did the money really arrive. Avara is designed so each answer has evidence your auditor can check.

Proof the goods arrived

Every delivery note asks the customer to tap "Sudah diterima" in WhatsApp, or carries a photo of the signed note. Invoices link to that evidence, and their date never comes before delivery.

No gaps, no deletions

Orders, delivery notes, invoices, receipts and credit notes are numbered without gaps. Issued documents can't be edited or deleted; corrections are new documents with a reason and an approver.

A trail that shows tampering

Every change records who, when, what it was before and after. Entries are chained so any edit breaks the chain, and a daily copy goes to locked storage in Indonesia for 10 years.

Cash you can reconcile

Bank imports are checked for balance continuity and reconciled monthly to the bank's official statement by two people. Field cash gets a numbered receipt sent to the customer, and giro counts only once it clears.

A month-end close that holds

A close checklist with preparer and reviewer: bank reconciliations, numbering gaps, cut-off, unapplied cash, credit notes, reconciliation to your general ledger. Closed months stay locked.

Read-only access for your auditor

Give your auditor a time-limited login, reports as of any date, and full exports with checksums for their own testing. AI only drafts; every sale shows who or what authorised it.

These controls are part of Avara's design from day one. Month-end close, auditor access and full exports roll out after the first pilot phase. Avara does not replace your auditor's procedures and has no independent assurance report yet.

Laws and standards we build to

Designed in line with Indonesian law and recognised standards.

Every requirement in Avara's specification is mapped to the rule it comes from, so your legal, finance and IT teams can check the reasoning instead of taking our word for it.

UU 27/2022 · PP 33/2026 Personal data protection (UU PDP)

Consent records in Bahasa Indonesia, opt-out honoured well within 3 x 24 hours, data subject requests on statutory timers, breach notice to you within 24 hours so you can meet the 3 x 24 hour deadline.

UU KUP Pasal 28 ayat 11 Bookkeeping records kept 10 years

Invoices, payments, delivery evidence and the change log are retained for 10 years, with a locked copy stored in Indonesia.

PSAK 115 Revenue timing you can defend

Invoice and revenue dates follow evidenced delivery, so the timing your accountant applies under PSAK 115 is backed by a record of when control passed to the customer.

Permenkominfo 5/2020 Registered electronic system (PSE)

Avara registers as a private electronic system operator before any customer goes live, and declares where data is stored and processed.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A Security controls mapped to all 93

We use the Annex A controls as our security checklist: access, logging, backups, supplier management, secure development. Not yet certified.

OWASP 2026 · WhatsApp policy Safe AI on WhatsApp

Threat-modelled against the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic and LLM Applications, and built to WhatsApp Business Policy: opt-in, a human one message away, no debt collection, no account numbers in chat.

"In line with" means our requirements are written to these rules and mapped one by one. It is not a certification or a legal opinion. Avara is not yet ISO 27001 certified and has no independent assurance report. As the owner of your customers' data, your company keeps its own obligations under UU PDP; we give you the tools and records to meet them.

Questions

The ones ops managers always ask.

Do we have to replace our ERP or accounting system?
No. Avara runs alongside your accounting app and handles the sales side: agreements, orders, invoices, payments and receivables. You load customers, price lists, terms and open invoices from a spreadsheet, and export invoices and payments back out for your books. Avara does not post journals or issue Faktur Pajak.
What happens if a customer sends the same message twice?
Nothing extra. WhatsApp can deliver a message more than once, so Avara deduplicates on the message ID before it does any work. The second copy is recognised and ignored, so you won't get two invoices for one request.
Can a payment be recorded twice?
No. Each bank statement line and each payment notification is recorded once, even if the same file is uploaded twice or a notification arrives twice. Whether money arrived is decided by your bank statement or the payment provider, never by the conversation.
What if the AI misreads a request or builds a bad action?
Every action is validated against a strict schema before it runs. If it doesn't match, it fails closed: nothing is executed, the attempt is logged, and Avara retries only that decision, never the steps that already succeeded. If it fails twice, the conversation is handed to your team and the customer is told someone will reply shortly.
Who on my team can see what?
Each person gets a role (owner, admin, manager, finance, sales admin, salesman or viewer) and a scope: the whole company, some branches, or only their assigned customers. It is enforced in the database, so removing someone takes effect immediately. Owners, finance and approvers use two-step login, and the person who requests a change can't approve it.
Does my team need to install an app?
No app store needed. Avara is a web app your team can add to the home screen on Android or iPhone, and it works on desktop too. Notifications open it directly. Staff can also ask the Avara assistant on WhatsApp for quick things like a customer's balance; sensitive actions such as changing credit limits are done in the app.
What happens when we run out of credits?
Your customers keep getting answers. You can top up anytime; if you don't, a short grace allowance kicks in and is billed on your next invoice. After that, Avara keeps replying to customers but pauses non-essential reminders and sends team notifications to the app only, until you top up or the new month starts.
Can we require approval before anything is sent?
Yes. Set rules by amount, customer or action: orders over the credit limit, price overrides, credit limit changes, manual payment matches. Anything that matches waits in the app for someone with approver rights. If no one decides in time, the action is cancelled, never auto-approved. New accounts start with approval on every over-limit order and every credit limit change.
Which payment methods are supported?
Bank transfer to your own accounts, matched from your bank statement. Giro and cheques, tracked until they clear. Cash collected by your salesmen or drivers, with a numbered receipt sent to the customer and the deposit matched to your bank. Down payments, held until they are applied to an invoice. Optionally Virtual Account, QRIS and e-wallet through DOKU. Payment details appear on a secure invoice page linked from the chat, not typed into the conversation. Until your DOKU merchant account is active, those payment links run in sandbox mode and are labelled as test links.
What does WhatsApp messaging cost?
Meta charges per message on the WhatsApp Business Platform, and since October 2026 that includes replies inside the 24-hour customer window. Avara keeps the count low by design: one reply per customer turn, no replies to "ok" or "makasih", one combined reminder per customer, and team notifications through the app first. Meta's fees are included in your package credits.Meta's fees are shown as a separate line on your invoice.
Will our auditor accept records from Avara?
Avara is designed so your auditor can test it: delivery evidence for each sale, numbering without gaps, documents that can't be edited, a tamper-evident change log, monthly bank reconciliation, period close and read-only auditor access with full exports. It does not replace your auditor's own procedures, and Avara does not yet have an independent assurance report on its controls (ISAE 3402 / SOC 1), so expect your auditor to test some controls directly. We provide a control description and help with their requests. Some controls depend on your team, such as reviewing access and signing the monthly close; we give you that list at onboarding.
How long are records kept, and where?
Invoices, payments, delivery evidence and the change log are kept for 10 years, in line with Indonesian bookkeeping rules, with a locked daily copy stored in Indonesia. Chat messages that aren't evidence for a document follow a shorter retention period set in your agreement.
Where is our data processed?
Your records are stored in a single database region named in your contract. To understand requests and read purchase orders or transfer receipts, the text and files involved are processed by our AI providers. Every provider that touches your data, and where it processes it, is listed on our Data Processing page.
How long does onboarding take?
Setup is guided: connect your number, import customers, price lists, terms and open invoices, set invoice numbering and approval rules, then run supervised alongside your current process. Two steps depend on outside approvals, Meta's review of your message templates and activation of your DOKU merchant account, so we start the pilot on everything else while those come through.
