What Avara does

Trust & safety

It touches money and invoices. It's built like it.

An assistant with write access is only useful if it's predictable. These aren't features you configure — they're how Avara is put together.

Tenants can't see each other

Isolation is enforced in the database itself, row by row — not by a filter in the app that someone might forget.

Signatures verified before anything happens

Every WhatsApp and payment notification is cryptographically verified before a single record is touched. Unsigned requests are rejected at the door.

PDFs are built in a sealed box

Invoice rendering runs isolated, with no internet access and no credentials — so a malicious document can't reach anything it shouldn't.

Payments can only land once

Payment notifications are idempotent — the same confirmation arriving twice still means one payment. The payment provider is the source of truth, never the chat.

Nothing happens off the record

Every tool execution is stored with its input, output and outcome — the same log your ops team reads when a customer disputes something.

A human is always one message away

When a request is unusual, ambiguous, or fails validation twice, Avara escalates to your team instead of guessing.

Questions

The ones ops managers always ask.

Do we have to replace our ERP or accounting system?
No. Avara sits in front of your records and writes into them — orders, invoices, payment status. Your ERP stays where it is. In the pilot we map the fields you actually need, not the whole chart of accounts.
What happens if a customer sends the same message twice?
Nothing extra. WhatsApp can deliver a message more than once, so Avara deduplicates on the message ID before it does any work. The second copy is recognised and ignored — you won't get two invoices for one request.
Can a payment be recorded twice?
No. Payment notifications are deduplicated both at the orchestration layer and at the database level, and the payment provider's notification — not the conversation — is always the source of truth for whether money arrived.
What if the AI misreads a request or builds a bad action?
Every action is validated against a strict schema before it runs. If it doesn't match, it fails closed: nothing is executed, the attempt is logged, and Avara retries only that decision — never the steps that already succeeded. If it fails twice, a human is looped in.
Who on my team can see what?
Access is scoped per tenant and enforced in the database. Staff only see the tenants they're members of, and every action is attributed in the audit log. Cross-tenant leakage is tested for in CI, not just assumed.
Can we require approval before anything is sent?
Yes. You can set thresholds by amount, by customer, or by action type. Anything above the line waits for a human in the dashboard; everything below goes out immediately.
Which payment methods are supported?
Virtual Account, QRIS and e-wallet through DOKU, with settlement in IDR. Payment instructions are delivered straight into the WhatsApp thread the customer is already in.
How long does onboarding take?
Most distributors are live on a single number in about a week: connect the number, configure invoice format and numbering, import parties and balances, approve outbound templates, then run a supervised week alongside your current process.
